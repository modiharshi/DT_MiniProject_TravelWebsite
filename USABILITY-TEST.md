# Usability test plan

**Status: proposed only. No real-user testing has been run.** Nothing in this file is a result. The
checks the builder ran themselves are listed in `VERIFY.md` under "What was actually tested" and are
developer checks, not usability findings.

## Goals
Find out whether a first-time traveller can get from the India map to a believable plan without help,
and whether the map, the group tools and the itinerary feel clear rather than clever.

## Who
5 to 8 people who plan trips: at least two who usually travel solo, two with a group or family, one who
uses a screen reader or keyboard only, and one on a phone. Not people who built it.

## Tasks (think aloud, no hints)
1. "You want a few days in Jaipur. Get to a plan." *(Success: reaches the itinerary unaided.)*
2. "Plan a long weekend in Manali for you and two friends who like different things." *(Success: each person has picks; can say what "Group overlap" shows.)*
3. "You are vegetarian and on a tight budget. Make sure the plan respects that." *(Success: sets budget and food, can point to a meal that matches.)*
4. "You don't like the second stop on day 1. Replace it." *(Success: uses "Not this one"; understands the message if nothing fits.)*
5. "Find out whether the café on day 2 is a real place." *(Success: opens details, notices the "example" label.)*
6. "Save this trip and open it again later." *(Success: finds My trips.)*
7. "Send this plan to someone." *(Success: uses Copy link or Print; can say what the link contains.)*
8. "Go back and change your budget." *(Success: uses Change my choices or Back; is not surprised by what resets.)*
9. Phone only: "Look at day 2 and find the evening plan."
10. Keyboard only: tasks 1, 3 and 4.

## What to observe
Time to first plan; wrong turns on the map; whether Back and Escape behave as expected; whether people
read the "example" and "estimate" labels; whether the compromise meter is understood; whether the
group vote buttons are discovered; any place where the animation made someone wait.

## Questions afterwards
- What did you think the budget bar measured?
- Did you believe the traveller notes were reviews? Why or why not?
- What would make you trust or distrust the plan?
- Anything you felt you could not skip?

## Success criteria (to agree before testing)
At least 80% complete tasks 1 to 4 unaided; nobody mistakes sample notes for reviews; nobody reports
being stuck on the map; keyboard and screen-reader participants complete task 1 without leaving the flow.

## Recording results
When tests are run, add a dated "Results" section below with participant count, task completion and
verbatim quotes. Do not edit the plan above to match the results.
